The world's biggest cargo airline survived, because its founder bet the company's last five thousand dollars on blackjack, in Las Vegas!
In April, nineteen seventy-three, Federal Express launched with fourteen small Falcon jets, out of Memphis! On that first night, they carried just one hundred eighty-six packages, to twenty-five cities! Fourteen jets, for one hundred eighty-six packages!
Fuel costs kept climbing, and the money kept vanishing! Then the company's cash fell to five thousand dollars! Nowhere near enough to keep fourteen jets in the air.
You see, founder Fred Smith did something no business school would ever teach! He flew to Las Vegas, sat down at a blackjack table, and turned that five thousand into twenty-seven thousand dollars!
One card table paid the bills, just long enough. Not a strategy. A lifeline! The jets kept flying, investors came back, and Smith raised eleven million dollars more! The tiny overnight company was alive!
And the idea itself? It started as a college paper Smith wrote at Yale. His grade was reportedly a C! Graded average, saved by a card game, and today, it is the world's biggest cargo airline!
