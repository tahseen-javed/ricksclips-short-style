The Hollywood sign was never about movies! It's a billboard for houses, and it was built to come down!
Los Angeles, nineteen twenty-three! A group of developers, including the publisher of the Los Angeles Times, is selling hillside lots in a neighborhood called Hollywoodland! So they write its name on the hill!
Thirteen letters, each about forty-five feet tall! Four thousand light bulbs! Twenty-one thousand dollars! Planned life: about a year and a half!
You see, nobody took it down! It just sat there, falling apart! Then in nineteen forty-nine, a deal saved it, with one condition: cut the last four letters!
~El! Ay! En! Dee!
So the most famous movie sign on Earth is a real estate ad with the ending chopped off! And it was never about movies!
