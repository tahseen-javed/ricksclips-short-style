The Eiffel Tower was built to be torn down! One wire on top saved it! Then it caught a spy!
Paris, eighteen eighty-nine! Gustave Eiffel gets a permit for just twenty years! Famous artists already call his tower "useless and monstrous!" After twenty years, the city can take it, and tear it down!
Eiffel needs a reason! A big one!
You see, Eiffel turns his tower into a giant antenna! In eighteen ninety-eight, a radio signal flies from the top to the Pahn-tay-on, four kilometers away! The army moves in! A permanent radio station follows! And Paris renews his permit for seventy more years! Then war comes! The tower listens to enemy radio! They call it "the big ear!"
It intercepts messages about a secret agent, code name H twenty-one! Her real name? Mata Hari!
Not bad for a tower that was built to be torn down!
