G T A Online's loading screen beat Rockstar for seven years! One player killed it!
Every time you joined, you waited. Six minutes!
Early twenty twenty-one! A programmer who calls himself Toast gets curious! He digs in to see what the game does during that wait!
You see, at startup the game reads one file! Ten megabytes! Nearly sixty-three thousand items! And for every item, it checks the whole list again! Almost two billion pointless checks!
So Toast writes his own small fix! Six minutes become under two! Seventy percent of the wait, gone!
Rockstar pays him ten thousand dollars, and promises to use his fix!
