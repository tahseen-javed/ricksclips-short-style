Lightning never strikes twice! It hit Roy Sullivan seven times, and he survived every single one!
Roy was a park ranger in Shenandoah National Park, Virginia! In nineteen forty-two, lightning hit him at a fire lookout tower and scorched his leg! Then nothing for twenty-seven years! Until nineteen sixty-nine!
A bolt came through his truck window, knocked him out cold, and burned off his eyebrows! Nineteen seventy: it found him in his own yard and seared his shoulder! Nineteen seventy-two: inside a ranger station! His hair caught fire, and he smothered it with wet towels!
Nineteen seventy-three: he drove away from a storm cloud! It caught him anyway, and his hair burned again! Nineteen seventy-six: his ankle! And in nineteen seventy-seven, while he was fishing, a bolt hit the top of his head and burned his chest and stomach! Then, on the way back, he ran into a bear!
You see, most people never get struck even once! But lightning hits whoever is outside when the storm arrives, and Roy's whole job was to be outside, in the mountains, through every storm, for decades! Guinness World Records verified all seven strikes! Nobody on record has survived more!
But here's the strangest part! The one time lightning hit his family, Roy was standing right beside his wife, helping her hang laundry! It hit her! And it skipped the one man it never seemed to miss!
