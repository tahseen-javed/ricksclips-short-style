Pinball was illegal in New York for thirty-four years, and one man ended the ban with a single ball!
In nineteen forty-two, Mayor Fiorello La Guardia declared pinball gambling! Police raided bars and bowling alleys and seized thousands of machines! La Guardia smashed them with a sledgehammer for the cameras, and the pieces were dumped into the city's waters!
His reason? He said pinball stole children's nickels and dimes! Their lunch money! Chicago and other cities banned it too! For three decades, a pinball machine in New York was contraband!
You see, the whole fight came down to one question! Is pinball luck, or skill? Luck meant gambling! Skill meant a game!
So in nineteen seventy-six, the industry hired a pinball expert named Roger Sharpe to prove it in front of the City Council! A suspicious councilman made him switch to a backup machine!
Sharpe pointed at the center lane and said the ball would go right there! He pulled the plunger! It went exactly there! The council voted unanimously to lift the ban!
Which means a city that banned gambling let one man bet pinball's entire future on a single ball!
