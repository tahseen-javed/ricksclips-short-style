It cost America almost four cents to make one cent! Every single time!
Not once! For nineteen straight years, the penny cost more than it was worth!
In twenty twenty-four alone, the U.S. Mint lost eighty-five million dollars making money! Literally making money!
And about three hundred billion pennies are still out there! In jars! Under couch cushions! On parking lot floors!
You see, one cent never changed! But the cost to make it, manage it and ship it kept climbing, to three point six nine cents a coin!
So on November twelfth, twenty twenty-five, in Philadelphia, America struck its last circulating penny! Then something strange happened!
The final pennies went to auction! Two hundred thirty-two sets! They sold for sixteen point seven six million dollars! One set alone went for eight hundred thousand dollars!
The coin that lost money for almost twenty years finally made a fortune the day it died!
All because it cost America almost four cents to make one cent!
