GTA 6 launches on November 19th. The first players got it on November 18th.

It was promised for fall 2025. Then May 26th, 2026. Then November 19th. Two delays.

It still became the most pre-ordered game in PlayStation Store history.

And the box on the shelf? No disc inside. Just a download code, for a game over 200 gigabytes.

You see, GTA 6 doesn't unlock at one moment. It unlocks at midnight local time, country by country. New Zealand hits midnight first. In London, that's 11 a.m. on November 18th. In New York, 6 a.m. Then the wave rolls west, one time zone at a time.

So after years of delays, launch day didn't fit inside one day. Players in New Zealand were already playing while most of the planet was still living November 18th.
