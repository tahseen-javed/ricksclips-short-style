A hole the size of a pizza swallowed an entire lake!
November twentieth, nineteen eighty! Texaco was drilling in Louisiana's Lake Pen-yur, just ten feet deep!
Then the fourteen-inch drill got stuck! The water started to spin!
A whirlpool swallowed the rig, eleven barges, and sixty-five acres of land!
A canal flowed backward into a hundred-fifty-foot waterfall!
You see, right under the lake sat a salt mine!
The drill punched straight into its tunnels, and the whole lake drained down!
All fifty-five miners ran, and every one escaped!
The canal refilled it with salt water from the Gulf!
Lake Pen-yur is still there! It's just not the same lake!
All because of a hole the size of a pizza!
