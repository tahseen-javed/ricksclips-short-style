A hole the size of a pizza swallowed an entire lake.

November 20, 1980. Texaco was drilling in Louisiana's Lake Peigneur, just 10 feet deep. Then the 14-inch drill got stuck. The water started to spin. A whirlpool swallowed the rig, 11 barges, and 65 acres of land. A canal flowed backward into a 150-foot waterfall.

You see, right under the lake sat a salt mine. The drill punched straight into its tunnels, and the whole lake drained down. All 55 miners ran, and every one escaped.

The canal refilled it with salt water from the Gulf. Lake Peigneur is still there. It's just not the same lake. All because of a hole the size of a pizza.
