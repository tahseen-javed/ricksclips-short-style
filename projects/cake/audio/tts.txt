German chocolate cake isn't German! It's named after a man called German, and it was born in Texas!
In the eighteen-fifties, a chocolate maker named Sam German created a sweet baking bar for the Baker's Chocolate Company! They named it after him: German's Sweet Chocolate!
A century later, in nineteen fifty-seven, a Texas homemaker sent her cake recipe, made with that bar, to a Dallas newspaper!
You see, readers went crazy for it! General Foods, which owned Baker's, sent the recipe to newspapers across the country! Sales of German's chocolate jumped seventy-three percent in one year! Then the recipes quietly dropped the apostrophe and the S!
German's chocolate cake became German chocolate cake, and America assumed it came from Germany! So the most German-sounding cake in America is pure Texas! And the only German in German chocolate cake is a man's last name!
