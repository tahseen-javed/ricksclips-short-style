A farm boy found a planet with a telescope built from an old car! Eighty-five years later, he flew there!
Kansas! A hailstorm wipes out the Tom-baw family's crops! Clyde, twenty-two, can't afford college!
So in nineteen twenty-eight he builds his own telescope! The parts? The crankshaft of a nineteen ten Buick and pieces of a cream separator!
He sketches Jupiter and Mars and mails the drawings to Lowell Observatory in Arizona! He wants advice! They send back a job offer!
You see, Lowell was hunting a mystery world called Planet X! They gave the farm boy the most tedious job in astronomy! Flip between two photos of the same sky, taken days apart, and find the one dot that moves! Among countless stars!
February eighteenth, nineteen thirty! He finds it! He is twenty-four! The world names it Pluto!
Clyde dies before anyone can visit it! But in two thousand six, NASA launches New Horizons, and inside a small capsule rides about an ounce of his ashes!
~July twenty fifteen! The spacecraft sweeps within seven thousand eight hundred miles of Pluto! Clyde finally arrives! There's just one problem!
Seven months after launch, astronomers voted Pluto out of the planets! His ashes left Earth for a planet and arrived at a dwarf! On paper, he lost his planet! In space, he finally reached it!
Because a farm boy found a planet!
