A president is in the Wrestling Hall of Fame. Not a pro wrestler. Abraham Lincoln.
Before the beard and top hat, Lincoln was a six-foot-four frontier wrestler. Legend says about 300 matches. The only recorded loss came in 1832, to a soldier named Thompson.
His most famous fight was in New Salem, against the town tough, Jack Armstrong. Crowds bet money. Armstrong walked away his friend.
You see, that friendship mattered. In 1858, Jack's son Duff was charged with murder. Lincoln defended him for free. A witness swore he saw everything by moonlight. Lincoln opened an almanac. The moon had almost set. Not guilty.
One wrestling match won a friend and saved his son. That is the man in the Wrestling Hall of Fame.
