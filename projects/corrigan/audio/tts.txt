Officials banned him from flying to Ireland! So he took off for California, and landed in Dublin!
His plane was a used nineteen twenty-nine Curtis Robin, that cost him about nine hundred dollars.
Inspectors called it too unsound to cross an ocean, and turned him down again and again!
Extra fuel tanks blocked his whole view forward.
Over the open sea, the cockpit floor flooded with gasoline!
He punched a hole in the floor with a screwdriver, and kept flying!
You see, Douglas Corrigan left New York at dawn on July seventeenth, nineteen thirty-eight.
His flight plan said Long Beach.
Twenty-eight hours and thirteen minutes later, he climbed out near Dublin, and said he had followed the wrong end of the compass needle! Clouds, he claimed. Bad visibility.
Officials suspended his license, but only until his ship docked back home! Then New York threw him a ticker-tape parade, bigger than Lindbergh's! They called him Wrong Way Corrigan!
He never once admitted it was on purpose.
Because officially, he was never flying to Ireland.
