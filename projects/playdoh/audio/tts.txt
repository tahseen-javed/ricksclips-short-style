Every kid in America has played with wallpaper cleaner! You just called it Play-Doh!
Cincinnati! Kew-tol Products, a small soap company! Back then, homes burned coal, and soot turned every wall gray! Kew-tol's best seller was a soft, doughy putty! You rolled it across the wallpaper, and the dirt came right off!
Then, after World War Two, America switched to gas and oil heat! No soot! No dirty walls! Washable vinyl wallpaper finished the job! Kew-tol's biggest hit was going down the drain!
You see, the rescue came from the family! Kay Zoo-fall was a nursery school teacher, and Joe Mac-Vicker's sister-in-law! She read that the putty could be used for art projects! She tried it with her class! The kids loved it! So she told Joe: stop selling it for walls! Sell it to kids! She even gave it a new name! Play-Doh!
In nineteen fifty-six, it hit a department store in Washington! Then Macy's! Then Captain Kangaroo started playing with it on TV!
More than two billion cans later, it's in the Toy Hall of Fame! Not bad for a product that was dying, because nobody needed to clean their walls! And every kid in America has played with wallpaper cleaner!
