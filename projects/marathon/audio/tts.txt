The first man across the Olympic finish line rode in a car! The winner ran on rat poison! And the poison was legal!
Saint Louis! August thirtieth, nineteen-oh-four! Thirty-two runners start the Olympic marathon in ninety-degree heat, on dirt roads! The organizer, James Sullivan, has an idea! Just one water stop! Mile twelve! He wants to study dehydration! On purpose!
Runners drop! A wild dog chases one man a mile off course! Félix Carvajal eats rotten apples from an orchard, takes a nap, and still finishes fourth! Then Fred Lorz crosses the line first! But Lorz quit around mile nine and hitched a ride! About ten miles! In a car! Disqualified!
The real winner is Thomas Hicks! You see, his trainers kept him moving with egg whites, brandy, and strychnine! Rat poison! He staggers over the line barely standing! Three hours, twenty-eight minutes! The slowest winning time in Olympic history! Only fourteen of thirty-two finish! And Hicks broke no rule!
Doping wasn't banned until nineteen-oh-eight! The poison was fine! The car was not! So the only cheater that day was the first man across the Olympic finish line!
