The more rats Hanoi killed, the more rats Hanoi had.
It's 1902. The French just built a shiny new sewer under the city. Rats love it. They climb straight up into the fanciest homes.
So the government makes an offer. One cent per rat. Just bring us the tail.
Hunters go wild. On June 21st, they turn in 20,112 tails. In one single day.
Victory? Then inspectors spot something strange. Rats. Alive. Healthy. Running through the streets... with no tails.
You see, a dead rat pays you once. A rat without a tail keeps breeding, and every baby grows a brand-new tail.
So hunters cut the tail and let the rat go. Outside the city, people built rat farms. Others smuggled rats in from other towns.
The bounty was scrapped. The rats stayed.
Because the city never counted dead rats. It counted tails.
And that's why the more rats Hanoi killed...
