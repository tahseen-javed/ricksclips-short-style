A rocket scientist invented the Super Soaker! By accident! In his bathroom!
Lonnie Johnson worked at NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory! He worked on Galileo, the probe sent to Jupiter! Serious work!
Nineteen eighty-two! At home, he's building a new cooling system that runs on water instead of Free-on! He hooks a homemade nozzle to his bathroom sink! Turns it on! A blast of water shoots clean across the room!
You see, most engineers would just mop the floor! Lonnie thought: kids would love this! He spent seven years turning that blast into a pressurized water gun! In nineteen eighty-nine, a toy company called Lar-a-mee finally licensed it!
It launched in nineteen ninety as the Power Drencher! They renamed it! Super Soaker! In about two years, it made two hundred million dollars! Total sales today? Close to a billion!
He set out to build a better way to cool things down! He nailed it! It just fits in a kid's hands! Because a rocket scientist invented the Super Soaker!
