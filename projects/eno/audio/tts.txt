The man who invented the stop sign never learned to drive a car!
William Phelps Eno! New York, eighteen sixty-seven! He's nine years old, stuck with his mother in a traffic jam! Not cars! About a dozen horses and carriages! And nobody, not the drivers, not the police, knows how to untangle it! He never forgets it!
In nineteen hundred, he writes an article demanding real traffic rules! It leads to New York's first written traffic regulations! Then he keeps going! The stop sign! One-way streets! Traffic circles! They call him the father of traffic safety! His foundation, started in nineteen twenty-one, still studies transportation today!
You see, Eno didn't need a steering wheel to see the problem! He was a horseman! His rules were written for carriages, and they worked so well that cars simply inherited them!
So every driver who stops at a stop sign is obeying a man who never drove! A man who started fixing traffic at nine years old, stuck behind a dozen horses!
