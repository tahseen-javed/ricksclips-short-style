Australia sent machine guns to war! The enemy had feathers! And the enemy won!
Campion, Western Australia! November, nineteen thirty-two! A drought pushes twenty thousand emus onto wheat farms run by World War One veterans! The wheat gets wrecked! The farmers beg for help! Defence Minister Pearce approves a military answer! The army sends three soldiers! Two Lewis machine guns! Ten thousand rounds! One week later, twenty-five hundred rounds are gone! The soldiers are pulled out! They come back! Same result! Nine thousand, eight hundred and sixty rounds fired! Just nine hundred and eighty-six birds claimed! Ten bullets for every emu! You see, the commander, Major Meredith, admitted it himself! Emus could face machine guns with the invulnerability of tanks! So the government stopped sending soldiers! It paid farmers a bounty per bird instead! No machine guns! No army! Six months in nineteen thirty-four! About fifty-seven thousand bounties claimed!
